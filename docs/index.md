# Formation Flight

Formation Flight is a free iOS timing aid for pilots flying a time-on-target. Pick a target and a time, and the app shows time to go, ETA, how early or late you are, and the groundspeed you need to arrive on time.

It is a situational-awareness aid only. It is not a primary navigation source and does not replace your aircraft's instruments, charts, or clearances.

Everything stays on your device. Location is used only while the app is open and is never stored or transmitted. The full statement is in the [privacy policy](privacy/).
